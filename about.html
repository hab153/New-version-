About Skyline AA‑1

Autonomous AI Sales Assistant

Skyline AA‑1 is an AI‑powered sales assistant that finds leads, writes personalized outreach, auto‑replies to emails, and schedules follow‑ups – so you can focus on closing deals, not manual work.

Designed for freelancers, startups, and sales teams, Skyline AA‑1 automates the entire outbound workflow. Tell the AI who you want to reach, and it finds real contacts, drafts custom emails, and manages your inbox – all in one unified interface.

What Skyline AA‑1 does

  • Lead generation – Describe your ideal customer (industry, location, job title) and the AI searches the web for real people and companies.
  • Smart email drafts – Each lead receives a personalised email based on their profile. You review, edit, and send in one click.
  • Auto‑reply – When a lead replies, the AI reads the message and responds according to your instructions (tone, discount, follow‑up questions).
  • Auto follow‑ups – Set a delay (1–7 days or custom) and the AI automatically sends a follow‑up email – no calendar reminders needed.
  • Unified inbox – All conversations with leads are stored in one place. See reply history, engagement scores, and unread notifications at a glance.

Why it's different

Most AI tools do only one thing: generate emails or chat. Skyline AA‑1 combines lead discovery, email automation, auto‑replies, and scheduled follow‑ups into a single workflow. It remembers your leads, your tone, and your instructions – so you never lose a conversation.

We also built tiered usage limits (Free, Go, Pro) to ensure fair access and scalability. Whether you're sending 5 emails a day or 100, Skyline AA‑1 adapts to your volume.

Who is it for?

  • Freelancers who want to automate client outreach and follow‑ups.
  • Startups that need to generate leads without a sales team.
  • Sales professionals who manage hundreds of conversations.
  • Founders who want to focus on product, not prospecting.

“Skyline AA‑1 turned my inbox into a sales machine. I closed three deals last month without writing a single follow‑up email manually.” – early user

Technology & privacy

We use OpenAI for AI generation, Nylas for secure email integration, and Flutterwave for payments. Your data is stored encrypted, and we never sell or share your leads. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Skyline AA‑1 is built for scale – with request queues, daily limits, and a modular architecture. Thousands of users can use it simultaneously without slowdown.
