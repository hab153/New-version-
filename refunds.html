Skyline may offer paid monthly plans. The applicable price, currency, features, limits, and billing period will be shown at checkout before you complete a purchase.

Unless the checkout page states otherwise, a paid subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period until it is cancelled.

3. How to cancel

You may cancel your paid subscription through the cancellation control provided in your Skyline account or by contacting:

skylineaa1company@gmail.com

When contacting us, use the email address connected to your Skyline account and include your account email and, if available, your transaction reference. Do not send your full card number, CVV, password, or other payment-secret information.

Cancellation stops future renewal. Unless we state otherwise or applicable law requires a different result, you may continue using paid features until the end of the current paid billing period.

Cancellation does not automatically create a refund for a billing period that has already started.

4. Refunds generally

Payments are generally non-refundable after a paid billing period begins, except where a refund is required by law or where one of the circumstances below applies.

We may consider a refund for:

  • a duplicate charge;
  • a payment that was processed after a valid cancellation was completed;
  • an unauthorized payment, after reasonable verification;
  • a significant technical failure that prevented reasonable use of the paid service; or
  • another situation where fairness or applicable law requires a refund.

Refunds are not normally provided for:

  • unused time after a customer chooses to stop using the service;
  • a failure to cancel before the next renewal;
  • a change of mind after using the paid features;
  • inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated lead information where the service was otherwise available;
  • AI output that the customer did not review or chose not to use;
  • emails that recipients did not open or answer; or
  • account suspension caused by violation of the Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy.

These exclusions do not remove any refund, cancellation, or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

To request a refund, email:

skylineaa1company@gmail.com

We may ask for additional information to verify the account and payment. We will review requests fairly and may ask questions about account usage, cancellation timing, or technical problems.

If a refund is approved, Skyline will initiate it through the applicable payment provider or payment method used for the transaction.

Some refunds may be returned in the settlement currency or converted by the payment provider or financial institution. Currency-conversion rates and bank charges may affect the amount received.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us promptly at skylineaa1company@gmail.com so we can review the transaction.

Please contact Skyline first when you believe a charge is incorrect, duplicated, unauthorized, or related to a technical problem. We will try to investigate and resolve legitimate issues.

Deleting your Skyline account does not necessarily cancel a paid subscription automatically unless the application clearly confirms that cancellation has occurred.

Before deleting an account, cancel any active subscription or contact skylineaa1company@gmail.com and request confirmation that future renewals have been stopped.

Skyline may retain limited payment, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, or legal records after account deletion where retention is required or reasonably necessary. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

Skyline may change its plan features, limits, prices, or billing structure as the service develops.
