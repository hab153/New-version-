Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 October 2026 Last updated: 21 October 2026

Skyline AI (“Skyline,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an online software service operated by Habeebullah, based in Nigeria. Skyline helps businesses and agencies research prospects, prepare sales outreach, manage conversations, and organize follow-ups.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Skyline collects, how we use it, which service providers help us operate the service, and the choices available to users.

Privacy contact: skylineaa1company@gmail.com

Skyline is currently in beta. Some features, providers, and data practices may change as the service develops. When they change materially, we will update this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create a Skyline account, we may collect:

  • username;
  • email address;
  • password credential;
  • account settings;
  • age eligibility information; and
  • information needed to authenticate and protect your account.

Skyline does not store passwords in readable form. Passwords are intended to be protected using secure one-way hashing, such as bcrypt.

Chat and AI information

We collect and store chats, prompts, instructions, messages, and other content that you submit to Skyline’s AI and chat features. This may include business information, sales plans, lead-research instructions, email drafts, and conversation history.

Lead and business information

Skyline may process information about businesses, prospects, and contacts, including:

  • company names;
  • websites;
  • industries;
  • locations;
  • names and job titles;
  • business email addresses;
  • public business information;
  • research results;
  • lead notes and qualification information;
  • outreach drafts; and
  • sales-workflow information.

Lead information may come from public sources, search or research services, user-provided information, and AI-assisted research. Skyline does not guarantee that lead information is complete, current, or accurate.

Do not use Skyline to collect or upload sensitive personal information unless it is necessary, lawful, and appropriate for your use of the service.

Email information

If you connect an email account through Skyline or Nylas, Skyline may process the information necessary to provide the email features you authorize. This may include:

  • email-account information;
  • message content;
  • sender and recipient information;
  • message dates and metadata;
  • drafts;
  • replies; and
  • conversation history.

Skyline may use this information to provide a smart inbox, prepare email drafts, suggest replies, manage approved outreach, and organize follow-ups.

Skyline is intended to request only the permissions needed for the email features being used. Users should review the permissions shown during email connection before authorizing access.

Payment and subscription information

If you purchase a paid plan, Skyline may receive and store information such as:

  • selected plan;
  • subscription status;
  • payment status;
  • transaction reference;
  • billing and renewal information;
  • refund information; and
  • chargeback or dispute information.

Flutterwave processes payment transactions on our behalf. Skyline does not intentionally store full card numbers, CVV codes, or other raw payment-card credentials.

Usage and technical information

We may collect information needed to operate and protect Skyline, including:

  • feature usage;
  • account activity;
  • plan and usage limits;
  • lead-search usage;
  • AI usage;
  • error reports;
  • security events;
  • IP address;
  • browser and device information; and
  • technical logs.

Browser storage

Skyline currently uses browser local storage for application and session-related information. Skyline does not currently use advertising cookies as part of the beta service.

If we add analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or similar tracking technologies, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • authenticate users;
  • provide lead discovery and business research;
  • provide chat and AI features;
  • prepare outreach drafts and reply suggestions;
  • provide email and inbox features authorized by users;
  • manage follow-ups and user-enabled automation;
  • process subscriptions, payments, renewals, refunds, and plan limits;
  • send account, security, billing, and support messages;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and unauthorized access;
  • diagnose errors and improve reliability;
  • respond to support, deletion, and data requests;
  • enforce our Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Skyline does not sell user information as a data-broker product.

3. AI processing

Skyline uses AI to process selected chats, prompts, lead-research instructions, company information, email content, and related context when needed to provide the feature requested by the user.

OpenAI processes selected content on Skyline’s behalf. Skyline intends to use customer content only to provide the requested Skyline feature, maintain security, provide support, and operate the service. Skyline does not intentionally use private customer content to train its own AI models.

AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. Users must review AI-generated research, lead information, messages, replies, and recommendations before relying on them or sending them to another person.

Skyline does not guarantee leads, contact details, replies, sales, revenue, conversion rates, or business results.

4. Automated emails and follow-ups

Skyline may provide features that draft replies, suggest follow-ups, or send follow-up messages when the user enables the relevant feature.

Users are responsible for:

  • reviewing and approving messages where approval is required;
  • ensuring that sender and business information is accurate;
  • complying with privacy, anti-spam, email-marketing, advertising, and telecommunications laws;
  • respecting unsubscribe and stop-contact requests; and
  • stopping automation when a recipient requests no further contact.

Users can contact Skyline at skylineaa1company@gmail.com if they need help stopping an automated feature.

5. Service providers

Skyline uses third-party providers to operate the service. These providers may process information on Skyline’s behalf, according to the services they provide and their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Current providers include:

  • OpenAI — selected AI processing;
  • Nylas — email-account connection and email-related processing;
  • Flutterwave — payment and subscription processing;
  • MongoDB Atlas — database storage; and
  • Render — backend hosting and application infrastructure.

Skyline’s public website may be hosted through Vercel. Vercel may process ordinary website request and hosting information when you visit the website.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a lawful request, prevent fraud or abuse, protect safety and security, enforce our agreements, or support a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

6. International processing and storage

Skyline operates from Nigeria, but its service providers may process information outside Nigeria. Skyline currently expects important application and database infrastructure to use United States-based cloud infrastructure, including MongoDB Atlas and Render, subject to the regions configured for the production service.

OpenAI, Nylas, Flutterwave, Vercel, and other providers may process information in other countries. By using Skyline, you understand that information may be transferred to and processed outside the country where you live.

7. Data retention

Skyline retains account information while an account is active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

During the beta, chats, leads, and related account content may remain available until the user deletes the content or account. Skyline intends to provide account-deletion and content-deletion controls as the product develops.

Export files are intended to remain available for up to seven days after they are created. After seven days, export files should be deleted unless they must be retained for security, legal, or support reasons.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, technical logs, payment records, accounting records, fraud-prevention systems, dispute records, or legal files. Those records may be retained for the period reasonably necessary or required by law.

8. Account deletion and data requests

Users may request account deletion through Skyline’s account-deletion feature, currently identified as delete-account.html, or by contacting skylineaa1company@gmail.com.

When an account is deleted, Skyline intends to delete or anonymize the associated account profile, chats, leads, email-related data, and other user content, subject to technical limitations and the retention exceptions described in this Privacy Policy.

Some information may be retained when necessary for payment reconciliation, tax or accounting records, fraud prevention, security, dispute handling, legal compliance, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

Users may also request access to, correction of, or an export of applicable personal information. We may ask for information needed to verify the requester’s identity.

9. Children

Skyline is intended for adults and business users. Users must be at least 18 years old to create a Skyline account.

Skyline is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to create accounts. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information without the required authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

If you believe a child has created a Skyline account, contact skylineaa1company@gmail.com.
