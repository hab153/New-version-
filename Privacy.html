Skyline AA‑1 · Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Skyline AA‑1 (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is an AI‑powered sales assistant that helps users find leads, send emails, automate replies, and manage follow‑ups. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information – username, email address, and hashed password.
  • Profile data – your name, industry, goals, and any other details you provide.
  • Conversation & lead data – chat messages, email drafts, lead contact information (name, email, company), and communication history.
  • Usage data – feature usage, session history, and daily request counts (to enforce fair use limits).
  • Payment data – we do not store credit card numbers; payments are processed by Flutterwave, and we store transaction references and subscription status.
  • Email integration – when you connect your email account via Nylas, we store OAuth tokens to send/receive emails on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI responses, draft emails, and provide lead suggestions.
  • To operate auto‑reply, auto‑follow‑up, and scheduled email features.
  • To manage your subscription, daily limits, and payments.
  • To improve and debug the service (analytics are anonymized where possible).
  • To communicate important service updates or security notices.

4. AI & Third‑Party Services

Skyline AA‑1 uses third‑party AI providers (OpenAI) to generate text, suggestions, and email content. Your inputs (messages, prompts) are sent to these services solely to produce the requested output. We also use:

  • Nylas – to connect your email inbox and send/receive messages.
  • Flutterwave – to process subscription payments.
  • MongoDB Atlas – to store your data securely.

These third parties have their own privacy policies, but we ensure data is transmitted securely and only used for the intended service.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data until you delete your account. Chat messages, lead conversations, and email logs are stored to provide historical context and improve the AI. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us or using the “Delete Account” feature inside the dashboard. After deletion, all associated messages, leads, and session data will be permanently removed.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations (court order, law enforcement).
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Skyline AA‑1 or its users.

7. Security Measures

We use industry‑standard security practices, including:

  • Encrypted database connections (TLS) and at‑rest encryption.
  • Password hashing with bcrypt.
  • JWT tokens for session management.
  • Rate limiting and request queuing to prevent abuse.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. Please use strong, unique passwords and protect your login credentials.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@skylineai.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

Skyline AA‑1 is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
